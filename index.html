❓ How to use Talking Robot

🗣️ Make it talk
Type text in the box, pick a language & voice below, then click Speak.
🎤 Make it listen
Click Listen and allow the microphone, then speak clearly. Turn on Auto Speak to have it answer automatically, or Continuous for hands-free mode.
😄 Change emotions
Click any emotion button under the robot to instantly change its face.
📡 Connect a micro:bit
Click Connect and pick your BBC micro:bit. Once connected, your words and emotions are sent to it automatically. Use the "Get Firmware Code" button to flash your micro:bit first.
💡 Tips
Use Chrome or Edge. Listen and BLE need http://localhost or https:// (not file:///). If a voice for your language is missing, install it in your device's system settings.
